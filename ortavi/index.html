Day trips from the house

The church, the valley and the mountain all start at our front door.

  1. Gergeti Trinity Church

    A 90-minute walk up the hill behind the house, or 15 minutes by jeep. Go before ten, ahead of the tour buses.

  2. The Truso Valley walk

    40 minutes by car, then a flat walk along the river past mineral springs and old towers. Take a packed lunch.

  3. Kazbek at sunrise

    On a clear morning the summit turns orange for ten minutes. The Kazbek-side rooms face it.

  4. Back at the house

    Dinner at 19:30 if you ask by noon, wool blankets on every bed and tea in the kitchen until late.
