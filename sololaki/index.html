The house, built in 1907

A wine merchant built it in 1907, with a carved staircase, painted ceilings and a balcony wide enough for dinner. It was flats for most of the last century.

We spent three years taking it back: the ceilings cleaned by hand, the staircase rebuilt from its own oak, the courtyard cleared down to the fig tree. There is no lift. Rooms are on three floors and the attic is up 54 steps.

Four walks of 12 minutes or less

Everything here is downhill from the house, and a taxi back up costs about 6 ₾.

  1. The sulphur baths, 9 minutes

    Book a private room at the baths for the evening. We call ahead for you and bring towels.

  2. Leghvtakhevi waterfall, 12 minutes

    Walk past the baths into the gorge. Go in the morning, before the tours.

  3. The cable car to Narikala, 7 minutes

    Up to the fortress for the whole city at once, then walk down through the botanical garden.

  4. The old town after dark, 10 minutes

    Ten minutes of lamp-lit streets, then back up the hill to After Dusk for the last glass.
